      How it's measured

      STEP 1654

      Laureates matched

      Every prize in Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine and Economic Sciences, 1901 to 2026, matched to an OpenAlex author profile using the papers published before the prize. Ambiguous matches are left out rather than guessed.

      STEP 2—

      Researchers at number 1

      All co-authors of every laureate, precomputed. Only peer-reviewed work counts: journal and conference articles, reviews and letters. Preprints, conference abstracts, corrections, books and retracted papers don't. Papers with more than 30 authors don't count either, so large collaborations don't make thousands of people neighbors. Your search reads your papers live and widens out to four steps.

      STEP 3Every link

      Identity-checked

      OpenAlex sometimes merges different people who share a name. A chain only counts if each person's two linking papers connect through their own record, by shared co-authors or the same affiliation. Chains that fail don't count.
